Sorleed is a complete design system and component toolkit engineered for fintech, commercial banking, wealth management, and double-entry ledgers. Source-first, zero-runtime lock-in, accessible by default, and equipped with native AI agent skills and machine-readable registry for autonomous agentic workflows.
